I am Dr. Divyansh Pathak, a Consultant Physiotherapist specializing in Neuro Rehabilitation, Sports Injury Recovery, and Advanced Pain & Movement Therapy. My clinical approach is rooted in evidence-based science, precision assessment, and personalized rehabilitation strategies designed to deliver measurable and lasting results.

I believe physiotherapy is not just about relieving pain — it is about restoring function, rebuilding confidence, and optimizing human movement. Every treatment plan I design is tailored to the individual, focusing on identifying the root cause of dysfunction rather than merely addressing symptoms.
